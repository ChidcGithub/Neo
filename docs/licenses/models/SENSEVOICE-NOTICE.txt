https://github.com/QwenAudio/SenseVoice/blob/586bbdb2b052f4fe19322581825228b6810a9fb3/README.md#license

into desktop software, rather than standalone model resale:
https://github.com/QwenAudio/SenseVoice/issues/286#issuecomment-4494732043
